TNet: AI-Powered Brain Tumor Diagnosis

About TNet

TNet is an innovative AI solution designed to revolutionize brain tumor diagnosis. Leveraging a convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture based on AlexNet, TNet offers accurate and efficient detection of brain tumors.

Our Mission

  • Empower medical professionals with advanced AI tools for rapid and accurate diagnosis.
  • Improve patient outcomes through early detection and effective treatment plans.
  • Drive innovation in healthcare with transformative AI technology.

Technical Overview

Model Architecture

The TNet model is based on AlexNet, featuring convolutional layers that extract spatial patterns to distinguish between healthy and tumor tissues. Key highlights include:

  • Feature extraction through convolutional layers with filters increasing up to 256.
  • Max pooling for dimensionality reduction and computational efficiency.
  • ReLU activation for non-linearity and faster training.
  • Fully connected layers with dropout regularization to prevent overfitting.

Training Details

  • Optimizer: Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD) with a learning rate of 0.003.
  • Epochs: Trained over 20 epochs with an 80/20 data split for training and validation.
  • Accuracy: Achieved 92.16% accuracy as per confusion matrix analysis.
